1. 运动通过肝 FGF21 驱动的小胶质细胞线粒体自噬减轻老年小鼠的神经炎症和认知能力下降。

   中文摘要：与衰老相关的神经炎症是认知能力下降的主要原因，而运动是对抗认知能力下降的有效非药物干预措施。然而，将外周适应运动与老年大脑中枢神经保护联系起来的分子机制仍不完全清楚。在这项研究中，我们使用老年雄性 C57BL/6J 小鼠进行了 16 周的跑步机运动，结果表明有氧运动可以改善认知功能并减轻海马神经炎症。通过生物信息学分析，我们确定成纤维细胞生长因子 21 (FGF21) 是一种运动诱导且抑制衰老的肝因子。值得注意的是，肝脏 FGF21 敲低消除了运动增强海马线粒体自噬的能力，及其对认知和神经炎症的有益作用。线粒体自噬的药理学阻断再现了 FGF21 功能的丧失，同样消除了运动引起的认知改善和神经炎症的减弱。这些发现表明 FGF21 的神经保护作用可能是通过促进线粒体自噬来介导的。从机制上讲，FGF21 激活小胶质细胞中的 AMPK 转录因子 EB (TFEB) 轴，以恢复溶酶体功能和线粒体自噬。小胶质细胞线粒体自噬的恢复伴随着衰老海马中胞质 mtDNA 积累的减少和 cGAS-STING 驱动的神经炎症的减弱。总之，我们的研究结果揭示了肝脑轴，运动诱导的肝脏 FGF21 通过该轴重塑衰老大脑中的小胶质细胞稳态，并将 FGF21 确定为年龄相关认知能力下降的潜在治疗靶点。
   英文摘要：Aging-associated neuroinflammation is a major contributor to cognitive decline, and exercise is an effective non-pharmacological intervention against it. However, the molecular mechanisms linking peripheral adaptations to exercise with central neuroprotection in the aged brain remain incompletely understood. In this study, using aged male C57BL/6J mice subjected to 16-week treadmill exercise, we show that aerobic exercise improves cognitive function and attenuates hippocampal neuroinflammation. Through bioinformatic analysis, we identified fibroblast growth factor 21 (FGF21) as an exercise-induced yet aging-suppressed hepatokine. Notably, hepatic FGF21 knockdown eliminated the capacity of exercise to enhance hippocampal mitophagy, along with its beneficial effects on cognition and neuroinflammation. Pharmacological blockade of mitophagy recapitulated the loss of FGF21 function, similarly abolishing the exercise-induced cognitive improvements and attenuation of neuroinflammation. These findings suggest that the neuroprotective effects of FGF21 may be mediated through the promotion of mitophagy. Mechanistically, FGF21 activated the AMPK-transcription factor EB (TFEB) axis in microglia to restore lysosomal function and mitophagy. Restoration of microglial mitophagy was accompanied by reduced cytosolic mtDNA accumulation and attenuated cGAS-STING-driven neuroinflammation in the aged hippocampus. Together, our findings reveal a liver-brain axis through which exercise-induced hepatic FGF21 reshapes microglial homeostasis in the aging brain, and identify FGF21 as a potential therapeutic target for age-related cognitive decline.

In mouse models of HE, oral administration of SP@RIF markedly reduced systemic ammonia levels and improved behavioral outcomes. Furthermore, SP@RIF enhanced intestinal barrier function, attenuated systemic inflammation and neuroinflammation, ameliorated cognitive impairments, and modulated the gut microbiota. Importantly, these therapeutic benefits were achieved without observable toxicity. These findings highlight SP@RIF as a potential therapeutic strategy for treating HE.
