中文摘要：背景：新生儿缺氧缺血（HI）是儿童死亡和神经发育障碍的主要原因。尽管低温治疗是迄今为止唯一临床确定的治疗方法，但对于相当一部分受影响的婴儿来说，结果仍然很差。过去，机制理解一直以大脑为导向，但肠道和免疫系统越来越被认为是脑损伤、恢复和神经发育的主动调节器。主体：肠道微生物群调节小胶质细胞成熟、髓鞘形成和血脑屏障完整性。新生儿 HI 会导致肠道菌群失调和屏障功能衰竭，并与神经炎症相关。首先，动物模型中的粪便微生物移植实验表明存在因果关系。临床数据证实了 HIE 婴儿的微生物扰动，尽管抗生素暴露和 NICU 环境代表了潜在的混杂因素。在免疫系统水平上，受HI影响的新生儿的外周先天性和适应性免疫反应失调，并且一些改变持续到学龄期。尽管在成人中风模型中微生物群与免疫相互作用已得到充分表征，但这些免疫反应的微生物群维度在很大程度上仍未被探索。候选治疗药物包括益生菌、母乳低聚糖和丁酸盐，每种药物都获得了初步的临床前支持，但尚未完成 HIE 的临床试验。结论：将新生儿 HI 重新定义为一种全身性肠脑免疫疾病，为辅助治疗和生物标志物发现开辟了新的可能性。进展需要纵向多组学临床队列、性别分层和疾病阶段解决的临床前分析以及对微生物组靶向干预措施的严格评估。

英文摘要：BACKGROUND: Neonatal hypoxia-ischemia (HI) is the leading cause of childhood mortality and neurodevelopmental disability. MAIN BODY: Gut microbiota regulate microglial maturation, myelination and blood-brain barrier integrity. Neonatal HI induces gut dysbiosis and barrier failure, associated with neuroinflammation. First, faecal microbiota transplantation experiments in animal models suggest a causal relationship. Clinical data corroborate microbial perturbations in HIE infants, though antibiotic exposure and the NICU environment represent potential confounders. At the level of the immune system, dysregulated peripheral innate and adaptive immune responses are well documented in HI-affected neonates, with some alterations persisting into school age. The microbiota dimension of these immune responses remains largely unexplored, despite well-characterized microbiota-immune interactions in models of adult stroke. Therapeutic candidates include probiotics, human milk oligosaccharides and butyrate, each with preliminary preclinical support but no completed clinical trials in HIE. CONCLUSION: Reframing neonatal HI as a systemic gut-brain-immune disease opens up new possibilities for adjunctive therapy and biomarker discovery. Progress requires longitudinal multi-omic clinical cohorts, sex-stratified and disease-phase-resolved preclinical analyses and rigorous evaluation of microbiome-targeted interventions.

While our previous work established that RIPK1 kinase inhibition promotes remyelination in acute demyelinating models, however, the precise mechanisms of RIPK1 in microglial activation remain unclear. Our findings reveal a novel RIPK1-Csf3-JAK2/STAT3 signaling axis governing microglial polarization and highlight its potential as a therapeutic target for demyelinating diseases.

Our findings identify the PLIN2-PGAM5 as a critical metabolic-immune pathway and suggest that targeting organelle-contact-mediated metabolic reprogramming represents a potential therapeutic strategy for CNS injury.

英文摘要：BACKGROUND: Perioperative neurocognitive disorder (PND) is a frequent postoperative complication in older individuals and is commonly linked to microglial activation. Tauroursodeoxycholic acid（TUDCA）, a pharmacological ER stress suppressor, was delivered bilaterally into dorsal hippocampal CA1 as a perioperative mechanistic probe. ER stress related proteins (GRP78, p-PERK, p-eIF2ɑ, p-IRE1ɑ, ATF4, and ATF6) in hippocampus were quantified by Western blotting. Microglial phenotypic signatures were assessed by Western blotting (CD86, iNOS, CD206, ARG-1) and double immunofluorescence (CD86/Iba-1 and CD206/Iba-1). CONCLUSIONS: These findings support a mechanistic link between hippocampal ER stress and the pro-inflammatory microglia bias linked to postoperative neuroinflammation and cognitive impairment in aged mice.

英文摘要：Multivesicular endosomes (MVEs) are endolysosomal compartments containing intraluminal vesicles (ILVs) that follow two alternative pathways: fusion with lysosomes, leading to ILV degradation, or fusion with the plasma membrane, resulting in ILV secretion as exosomes. Acting as GTPase-activating proteins, these proteins drive RAB11A inactivation and dissociation from endolysosomes. This reduces recruitment of the RAB11A effector exocyst complex, preventing MVE fusion with the plasma membrane. These findings thus define a BORC-ARL8-TBC1D9/TBC1D9B axis that further attenuates exosome secretion by blocking MVE-plasma membrane fusion.
