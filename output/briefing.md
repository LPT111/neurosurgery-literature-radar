英文摘要：Alzheimer's disease (AD) is now recognized as a biologically heterogeneous syndrome in which amyloid-β pathology, Tau-related alterations, and neuroinflammation are key interconnected processes. A distinction is made between biomarkers of core AD pathophysiology and those specific to neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration. An emphasis is placed on the clinical utility of plasma p-tau217 in combination with Aβ42/Aβ40, GFAP, NfL, sTREM2, YKL-40, and neuroinflammation imaging. With respect to therapeutics, an effort is made to focus on the need to harness desirable inflammatory pathways while specifically targeting deleterious processes rather than adopting an overarching anti-inflammatory approach. Single-cell, spatial, multi-omics, and biomarker-driven approaches may enable patient stratification based on underlying pathophysiology, allowing for stage-specific combination therapeutic strategies. Overall, studies presented in this review highlight a precision-neuroimmunology concept in which neuroinflammatory processes are both triggers and effectors of disease pathology and can be selectively modulated for clinical benefit.

英文摘要：Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a neurodegenerative disorder with limited disease-modifying treatment options. Melatonin, an endogenous hormone, has been shown to have therapeutic effects on amyloid deposition, oxidative stress, and neuroinflammation in AD. Eight-month-old female 5xFAD transgenic mice were treated with melatonin (10 mg/kg) for 4 weeks. Collectively, these findings suggest that melatonin ameliorates behavioral deficits in 5xFAD mice through both neurogenesis-independent (reduced Aβ plaque accumulation, Trem2-microglial activation, neurotransmission restoration, and metabolic remodeling) and hippocampal neurogenesis-dependent mechanisms. Overall, melatonin holds therapeutic potential for attenuating the neuropathology and behavioral deficits in AD.

中文摘要：轻度创伤性脑损伤 (mTBI) 通常会产生后遗症，使安全参与有氧运动变得复杂。尽管劳力评估已被用于评估运动耐量，但使用测试衍生指标来指导社区成年人有氧运动干预强度的可行性数据尚未提供。这项对更广泛的试点随机对照试验的二次分析检验了改良的运动自行车测试 (MEBT) 是否可用于有效地个体化受监督的虚拟有氧运动，而不会加剧 mTBI 患者的症状。在过去 12 个月内诊断出 mTBI 的成年人（18-55 岁）完成了 MEBT，并随机接受为期 12 周的监督虚拟有氧运动干预或主动平衡控制。那些被随机分配到有氧运动组的人在 MEBT 衍生症状阈值或意志力衰竭的 HR 的 65-80% 下进行锻炼。描述性会议结果、Spearman 相关性和 Fisher z 检验评估了 (1) 症状阈值 HR 干预指标的可行性，(2) HR-症状关系随时间的变化，以及 (3) mTBI 相关混杂因素对 HR-症状关联的影响。参与者完成了 95.6% 的有氧运动，仅 3.8% 的有氧运动出现症状恶化（增加≥3 个单位）。随着时间的推移，12 名参与者中有 10 名的 HR 症状严重程度关联性降低或呈负相关。此外，Fisher 的 z 检验显示混杂变量并没有有意义地改变 HR 与症状的关联。亚症状阈值运动课程限制了症状恶化，解开了心率与症状的关系，并随着时间的推移提高了运动耐量。这些发现强调了 mTBI 后进行体力症状引导的虚拟有氧运动的可行性，并支持进行更大规模的疗效试验。

英文摘要：Mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI) often creates a sequela of symptoms that complicate safely participating in aerobic exercise. Although exertional assessments have been used to evaluate exercise tolerance, data on the feasibility of using test-derived metrics to guide the intensity of aerobic exercise interventions in community-dwelling adults is yet to be presented. This secondary analysis of a wider pilot randomized control trial examined whether a modified exertional bike test (MEBT) could be used to effectively individualize supervised virtual aerobic exercise without exacerbating symptoms in individuals with a mTBI. Adults (18-55 years) diagnosed of mTBI within the previous 12 months completed the MEBT and were randomized to either a 12-week supervised virtual aerobic exercise intervention or active balance control. Descriptive session outcomes, Spearman correlations, and Fisher's z tests assessed (1) the feasibility of symptom-threshold HR intervention metrics, (2) changes in the HR-symptom relationship over time, and (3) the influence of mTBI-related confounders on HR-symptom associations. Participants completed 95.6% of aerobic sessions, with symptom exacerbation (≥3-unit increase) reported in just 3.8% of sessions. Ten of 12 participants displayed a decreased, or negative, HR-symptom severity association over time. In addition, Fisher's z tests revealed confounding variables did not meaningfully modify the HR-symptom association. Sub-symptom-thresholded exercise sessions limited symptom exacerbation, uncoupled the HR-symptom relationship, and improved exercise tolerance over time. These findings highlight the feasibility of exertional symptom-guided virtual aerobic exercise following mTBI and support progression towards larger efficacy trials.
