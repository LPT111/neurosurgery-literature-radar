英文摘要：BACKGROUND: Glioblastoma (GBM) remains a highly lethal brain tumor with limited therapeutic options and pervasive resistance to standard treatments. METHODS: We employed a range of preclinical GBM models-including 2D cultures, 3D spheroids, patient-derived organoids (PDOs), and multicellular 3D bioprints-to evaluate the effects of the CDK2/9 inhibitor fadraciclib in comparison to the standard-of-care temozolomide. Fadraciclib exhibits multifaceted anti-tumor activity and spares nonmalignant cells of the central nervous system, supporting its further development in therapies that target transcriptional and immune pathways, as well as quiescent tumor cell populations.

英文摘要：Galectin-3 (Gal3) acts as an intracellular sensor of lysosomal damage and a key driver of microglial activation and neuroinflammation in multiple neurodegenerative disorders. Transcriptomic profiling further identified Gal3 as a central regulatory hub within Huntington's disease-associated networks that were corrected upon treatment. Computational docking and molecular dynamics simulations further supported an allosteric binding mode distinct from the canonical glycan-binding domain of Gal3. Together, these findings establish berbamine hydrochloride as the first blood-brain barrier-penetrant small molecule inhibitor targeting intracellular Gal3 with therapeutic benefit in Huntington's disease. Furthermore, the puncta-based screening strategy that we developed provides a robust platform for discovering intracellular modulators of Gal3 relevant to a wide range of neurological diseases.
