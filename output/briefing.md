## 1. 阿尔茨海默病中的 ACOD1-衣康酸轴：免疫代谢机制和治疗机会。

中文摘要：阿尔茨海默病 (AD) 的特点是进行性认知能力下降，与淀粉样蛋白 - β (Aβ) 和 tau 病理学、慢性神经炎症、线粒体功能障碍、氧化应激和神经胶质细胞代谢重编程之间的相互作用有关。越来越多的证据表明免疫代谢失调是 AD 发病机制的关键因素，强调乌头酸脱羧酶 1 (ACOD1)/衣康酸途径是一个有前途的治疗靶点。衣康酸是 ACOD1 在免疫激活过程中由顺乌头酸产生的内源性代谢产物，通过多种机制调节炎症和代谢信号传导，包括琥珀酸脱氢酶抑制、KEAP1-Nrf2 抗氧化途径激活、核因子-κB 信号传导抑制以及 NLRP3 炎症小体激活的调节。通过这些机制，衣康酸可以减轻氧化应激，调节线粒体功能，调节小胶质细胞和星形胶质细胞的激活，并促进神经免疫稳态。新出现的临床前证据进一步表明，衣康酸及其衍生物可以减轻 Aβ 相关的神经炎症，调节 tau 相关的病理过程，保护突触蛋白，包括突触素和突触后密度蛋白 95 (PSD-95)，并改善 AD 实验模型的认知表现。这篇综述批判性地研究了 ACOD1-衣康酸信号传导的生物合成和分子机制，特别强调其在神经炎症、淀粉样蛋白和 tau 病理学、突触功能障碍和胶质细胞免疫代谢重编程中的参与。它还讨论了多组学方法的进展、衣康酸衍生物的开发以及潜在的治疗组合。最后，强调了关键的转化障碍，包括血脑屏障渗透、药代动力学特性、代谢稳定性、细胞特异性和临床验证的需要，以定义将基于 ACOD1-衣康酸酯的疗法转化为潜在的 AD 疾病缓解策略的未来方向。

英文摘要：Alzheimer's disease (AD) is characterized by progressive cognitive decline associated with the interplay between amyloid-β (Aβ) and tau pathology, chronic neuroinflammation, mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, and metabolic reprogramming of glial cells. Increasing evidence identifies immunometabolic dysregulation as a key contributor to AD pathogenesis, highlighting the aconitate decarboxylase 1 (ACOD1)/itaconate pathway as a promising therapeutic target. Itaconate, an endogenous metabolite generated from cis-aconitate by ACOD1 during immune activation, regulates inflammatory and metabolic signaling through several mechanisms, including succinate dehydrogenase inhibition, activation of the KEAP1-Nrf2 antioxidant pathway, suppression of nuclear factor-κB signaling, and modulation ofNLRP3 inflammasome activation. Through these mechanisms, itaconate may attenuate oxidative stress, modulate mitochondrial function, regulate microglial and astrocytic activation, and promote neuroimmune homeostasis. Emerging preclinical evidence further suggests that itaconate and its derivatives may attenuate Aβ-associated neuroinflammation, modulate tau-related pathological processes, preserve synaptic proteins, including synaptophysin and postsynaptic density protein 95 (PSD-95), and improve cognitive performance in experimental models of AD. This review critically examinesthe biosynthesis and molecular mechanisms of ACOD1-itaconate signaling, with particular emphasis on its involvement in neuroinflammation, amyloid and tau pathology, synaptic dysfunction, and glial immunometabolic reprogramming. It also discusses advances in multi-omics approaches, the development of itaconate derivatives, and potential therapeutic combinations. Finally, key translational barriers, including blood-brain barrier penetration, pharmacokinetic properties, metabolic stability, cellular specificity, and the need for clinical validation, are highlighted to define future directions for translating ACOD1-itaconate-based therapies into potential disease-modifying strategies for AD.

英文摘要：OBJECTIVE: This study investigated the effects of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) on functional recovery in photothrombotic (PT) stroke mice and whether rTMS promotes neurorepair by modulating microglial phagocytosis. Microglial activation, synaptic phagocytosis, neuronal injury and the levels of tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α), interleukin-6 (IL-6), and interleukin-1β (IL-1β) were evaluated. Differentially expressed proteins and core regulatory pathways were analyzed. RESULTS: rTMS treatment significantly increased body weight, prolonged the latency to fall and shortened the balance beam crossing time, reduced foot faults, and improved the discrimination index in PT mice. rTMS reduced the number of Iba1-positive microglia and CD68-positive cells, attenuated microglial synaptic phagocytosis and alleviated neuronal loss and apoptosis. rTMS significantly decreased the levels of TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-1β in the brain. A total of 416 differentially expressed proteins were identified between the PT and PT + rTMS groups. The differentially expressed proteins were significantly enriched in microglial phagocytosis pathways and related signaling pathways. CONCLUSIONS: rTMS significantly ameliorates neurological deficits and cognitive impairment in PT stroke mice. The underlying mechanism may involve the inhibition of excessive microglial activation, attenuation of microglia-mediated synaptic phagocytosis and alleviation of neuroinflammation. Proteomic analysis reveals that rTMS modulates the expression of proteins associated with microglial phagocytosis-related signaling pathways, which may contribute to the observed post-stroke neurorepair effects.

英文摘要：Plekha7 associates with p120 catenin and localizes at apical cell-cell junctions, where it has been implicated in junction stabilization. However, its in vivo function and underlying mechanisms of action remain unclear. Here, we show that constitutive Plekha7 loss in mice disrupts retinal organization, reduces retinal thickness, and induces retinal pigment epithelium nuclear layer multinucleation, microglia infiltration, and photoreceptor loss. Plekha7 deficiency reduces the accumulation of cadherin-catenin complexes at apical junctions without altering overall cadherin levels, indicating a defect in junctional maintenance. Mechanistically, PLEKHA7 regulates cadherin trafficking by limiting levels of endocytosed E-cadherin, thereby maintaining junctional cadherin pools. Notably, these effects are largely independent of p120 catenin. In addition, further Plekha7 roles in the regulation of centrosome, cilia, microtubule dynamics, and cytokinesis likely contribute to the phenotypic changes induced by Plekha7 knockout. These findings support a role for Plekha7 in cadherin trafficking and epithelial organization with potential relevance to retinal disease.

Survival after intensive care does not always mean full recovery. 中文摘要：背景：多方通信要求演讲者根据不同知识水平的多个收件人调整他们的信息。此类针对典型人群和临床人群的多方受众设计的研究仅集中于口语。在当前的研究中，我们扩展了这一分析，以研究有或没有 TBI 的演讲者如何调整他们的手势和眼神行为以适应多方观众设计。方法：30 名患有中重度 TBI 的成年人和 30 名未受伤的参与者与两名匹配者一起玩参考交流游戏。在排序阶段，他们在四轮中重复描述抽象图像。在随后的测试阶段，参与者再次向两组匹配者描述图像，这些匹配者要么知识渊博，要么天真，要么混合。我们使用基于视频的运动跟踪来量化具有不同知识水平的听众群体的手势形式运动学的调整，并手动注释对匹配器的注视事件。结果：未受伤的参与者表现出了跨模式的多方观众设计的协调努力，最大限度地提高了他们的口头和手势描述的信息量，并使用凝视来监测天真的和混合群体条件下的理解。相比之下，尽管口语调整减少，患有 TBI 的参与者仍表现出对手势形式运动学特征的保留调整，并将目光适当地分配给天真的匹配者，这表明对合作伙伴特定知识表示的认识。患有 TBI 的参与者做出手势的频率明显高于未受伤的同龄人。结论：结果强调了多模式方法研究认知沟通障碍的重要性，以及对多方受众设计破坏背后的认知机制的新见解。

英文摘要：BACKGROUND: Multiparty communication challenges speakers to adjust their messages for multiple addressees with varying knowledge levels. Such studies of multiparty audience design in both typical and clinical populations have narrowly focused on spoken language only. In the current study, we extend this analysis to examine how speakers with and without TBI adjust their gesture and eye gaze behaviors for multiparty audience design. METHOD: 30 adults with moderate-severe TBI and 30 non-injured participants played a referential communication game with two Matchers. In the Sorting phase, they described abstract images repeatedly across four rounds. In a subsequent Test phase, participants described the images again to groups of two Matchers who were either both knowledgeable, both naïve, or mixed. We used video-based motion tracking to quantify adjustments in gesture form kinematics for groups of listeners with different levels of knowledge and manually annotated gaze events toward Matchers. RESULTS: Non-injured participants demonstrated a coordinated effort for multiparty audience design across modalities, maximizing the informativeness of their spoken and gestural descriptions and using gaze to monitor understanding in naïve and mixed group conditions. In contrast, despite showing reduced spoken language adjustment, participants with TBI showed preserved adjustment of kinematic features of gesture form and appropriately allocated gaze to naïve Matchers, signaling awareness of partner-specific knowledge representations. Participants with TBI produced gestures at significantly higher rates than non-injured peers. CONCLUSIONS: The results highlight the importance of a multimodal approach to studying cognitive-communication disorders and new insights into cognitive mechanisms underlying disruptions to multiparty audience design.
