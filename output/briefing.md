## 1. 肝细胞来源的外泌体 miR-122 介导酒精暴露的阿尔茨海默病小鼠的肝脑串扰和神经炎症。

中文摘要：神经炎症和小胶质细胞激活是阿尔茨海默病 (AD) 和过量饮酒的常见特征。据报道，酒精会引起肝损伤和炎症，并且 AD 患者血清转氨酶发生改变。小细胞外囊泡 (sEV) 及其负载物正在成为 AD 和酒精性肝损伤中细胞间通讯的介质。然而，饮酒对 AD 进展的影响仍有待阐明。在这项研究中，我们评估了反复酗酒对 APP/PS1 AD 小鼠模型肝脏和大脑的影响。暴露于酒精的 APP/PS1 小鼠血清丙氨酸氨基转移酶水平升高，表明对照组不存在肝损伤，并且肝脏和大脑中促炎细胞因子表达增加。酒精增加了 AD 小鼠和小鼠肝细胞中 sEV 的生物发生和释放。这些 sEV 富含成熟的 miR-122-5p，其在肝细胞中占主导地位。在体内，在暴露于酒精的 AD 小鼠的大脑中检测到 miR-122-5p，表明 EV 介导的 miR-122-5p 转移到大脑。体外证实了小胶质细胞对肝细胞来源的 sEV 的摄取。来自酒精处理的肝细胞的 sEV 使 BV-2 小胶质细胞对 LPS 刺激敏感，显着增强 CCL2、CXCL2、CCL3 和 TNF-α 的产生。用 miR-122-5p 模拟物转染小胶质细胞，部分重现了来自酒精暴露肝细胞的 sEV 的促炎致敏作用，而 miR-122-5p 抑制剂递送可逆转这种作用。 ADAM10 是 miR-122-5p 的靶标，在酒精暴露后，野生型和 AD 大脑中的 ADAM10 均减少，并具有细胞特异性效应。这些结果表明，酒精诱导的肝细胞来源的 sEV 可能通过 miR-122-5p 调节肝到脑的信号传导，并导致 AD 中的神经炎症。

英文摘要：Neuroinflammation and microglial activation are common features in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and excessive alcohol consumption. Alcohol induces liver damage and inflammation, and altered serum aminotransferases have been reported in patients with AD. Small extracellular vesicles (sEVs) and their cargo are emerging as mediators of intercellular communication in AD and alcohol-induced liver injury. However, the effects of alcohol use on AD progression remain to be elucidated. In this study, we evaluated the effects of repeated binge drinking on the liver and brain in the APP/PS1 mouse model of AD. Alcohol-exposed APP/PS1 mice showed elevated serum alanine aminotransferase levels, indicating liver injury absent in controls, along with increased pro-inflammatory cytokine expression in both liver and brain. Alcohol increased the biogenesis and release of sEVs in AD mice and in mouse hepatocytes. These sEVs were enriched in mature miR-122-5p that is predominant in hepatocytes. In vivo, miR-122-5p was detected in the brain of alcohol-exposed AD mice suggesting EV-mediated miR-122-5p transfer to the brain. Microglial uptake of hepatocyte-derived sEVs was confirmed in vitro. sEVs from alcohol-treated hepatocytes sensitized BV-2 microglia to LPS stimulation, significantly enhancing CCL2, CXCL2, CCL3, and TNF-α production. Transfection of microglia with a miR-122-5p mimic partially recapitulated the pro-inflammatory sensitization by sEVs from alcohol-exposed hepatocytes, which was reversed by miR-122-5p inhibitor delivery. ADAM10, a miR-122-5p target, was reduced in both wild-type and AD brains after alcohol exposure, with cell-specific effects. These results suggest that alcohol-induced hepatocyte-derived sEVs may modulate liver-to-brain signaling via miR-122-5p and contribute to neuroinflammation in AD.

英文摘要：The diagnosis of traumatic brain injury (TBI) remains heavily constrained by the limitations of conventional imaging techniques in visualizing the pathological events that determine the disease progression and outcome. To address this clinical challenge, we report a manganese-doped near-infrared quantum dot (QD) to intrinsically integrate the highly sensitive optical imaging with clinical compatible magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Taking the advantages of optical imaging powered by whole-brain clearing and cellular resolution three-dimensional (3D) panoptic imaging, in combination with the systematic in vitro cell studies on different types of cells involved in TBI, we disclosed the interpretable pathology reflected by TBI imaging through the highly sensitive fluorescence of magnetic QDs, i.e., after systemic administration, the nanoprobes were extravasated through the compromised blood-brain barrier and then selectively phagocytosed by activated microglia, which subsequently migrated into the regions rich in dead neurons and neuronal debris. This microglia-mediated tropism decodes the signal origin of MRI enhanced by the nanometer-sized contrast agent, thereby transforming the MRI from an anatomical tool into a pathological reporter. A biocompatible Fe3O4 nanoparticle of similar size was also compared with the magnetic QD mentioned above for validating the translational merits of nanoparticles in MRI diagnosis of TBI.

中文摘要：小胶质细胞（MG）参与神经炎症反应已被广泛证明。最近的研究表明，饮食限制会抑制小胶质细胞的活性。然而，食物剥夺（FD）引起的体温过低和小胶质细胞改变之间的关系仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们检测了 FD 对大鼠海马、丘脑、下丘脑和大脑皮层体温 (Tb) 和 Iba1 免疫反应性 (Iba1-ir) 小胶质细胞的影响。在整个实验期间监测 Tb，并在 FD 3 天后评估小胶质细胞形态、细胞数量及其与 Tb 的相关性。结果表明：（1）FD在暗相期间显着降低了Tb，但在亮相期间没有显着降低Tb； (2) FD 还降低了小胶质细胞密度，并诱导了与所有检查的大脑区域中小胶质细胞激活减少一致的形态变化； (3)Tb与脑区小胶质细胞密度呈正相关，但大脑皮层无显着相关性。这些发现表明，FD 抑制多个大脑区域的小胶质细胞活化，这与 Tb 降低有关，这表明对 FD 的代谢适应可调节神经免疫稳态，并可能有助于减轻神经炎症的机制。

英文摘要：The involvement of microglia (MG) in neuroinflammatory responses has been demonstrated extensively. Recent studies have suggested that dietary restriction suppresses microglial activity; however, the relationship between food deprivation (FD)-induced hypothermia and microglial alterations remains unclear. Tb was monitored throughout the experimental period, and microglial morphology, cell number, and their correlation with Tb were evaluated following up to 3 days of FD. The results demonstrated that (1) FD significantly reduced Tb during the dark phase, but not during the light phase; (2) FD also decreased microglial density and induced morphological changes consistent with reduced microglial activation in all brain regions examined; (3) there was a positive correlation between Tb and microglial densities in brain regions, except for the cerebral cortex, which showed no significant correlation. These findings demonstrate that FD suppresses microglial activation in multiple brain regions in association with reduced Tb, suggesting that metabolic adaptation to FD modulates neuroimmune homeostasis and may contribute to mechanisms that attenuate neuroinflammation.

中文摘要：孤立性严重创伤性脑损伤 (TBI) 与高死亡率和长期发病率相关，尤其是在低收入和中等收入国家 (LMIC)。尽管在实验环境中使用药物和非药物疗法取得了有希望的结果，但没有临床试验证明有强大的疗效。我们的目的是研究抗分泌因子 (Salovum®) 对患有严重 TBI 的成年患者 30 天死亡率的影响。这是一项前瞻性、双盲、安慰剂对照 2 期试验，于 2017 年 9 月至 2022 年 2 月在南非开普敦的三级创伤中心（大学医院）进行（www.clinicaltrials.gov：NCT03339505）。参与者是患有孤立性严重 TBI（格拉斯哥昏迷评分 6-8）的成年人，接受颅内压 (ICP) 监测和神经重症监护。参与者通过鼻胃管服用Salovum®（富含活性化合物抗分泌因子的冻干蛋黄粉）或安慰剂（冻干蛋黄粉），根据体重（10-17克/4小时）给药，并在受伤后32.9小时±10.7小时（平均值±标准差）开始。服用研究药物或安慰剂直至 ICP 监测仪被移除或最多 5 天。该试验的主要结果是 30 天死亡率，次要结果指标是治疗强度水平 (TIL) 和 ICP。包括 100 名年龄在 18-61 岁之间的参与者，其中治疗组和对照组分别有 49 名和 51 名患者。大多数患者为男性 (96%)，平均年龄为 33 岁。治疗组的 30 天死亡率为 20%，对照组为 39%，相对风险比为 0.52（置信区间：0.271-0.997），P 值为 0.040（卡方检验）。两组的 TIL 和 ICP 相似。没有发现明显的不良事件。抗分泌因子显着降低了 LMIC 环境中严重 TBI 的 30 天死亡率。需要进一步研究抗分泌因子的作用机制。抗分泌因子的安全性、给药简便性和显着疗效支持多中心随机对照试验的探索。

英文摘要：Isolated severe traumatic brain injury (TBI) is associated with high mortality and long-term morbidity, especially in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). Despite promising results using pharmacological and non-pharmacological therapies in the experimental setting, no clinical trials have demonstrated robust efficacy. We aimed to investigate the effect of antisecretory factor (Salovum®) on 30-day mortality in adult patients with severe TBI. This was a prospective, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial, conducted from September 2017 to February 2022 in a tertiary trauma centre (university hospital) in Cape Town, South Africa (www.clinicaltrials.gov: NCT03339505). The participants were adults with isolated severe TBI (Glasgow Coma Score 6-8), receiving intracranial pressure (ICP) monitoring and neurointensive care. Participants were administered either Salovum® (freeze-dried egg yolk powder enriched with the active compound antisecretory factor) or placebo (freeze dried egg yolk powder) via the nasogastric tubing, dosed according to weight (10-17 g/4 h) and initiated at 32.9 h ± 10.7 h (mean ± standard deviation) post-injury. Study drug or placebo were administered until the ICP monitor was removed or for a maximum of 5 days. The primary outcome of this trial was 30-day mortality and secondary outcome measures were therapy intensity level (TIL) and ICP. One hundred participants aged 18-61 were included, with 49 and 51 patients in the treatment and control groups, respectively. Most patients were male (96%) and the mean age was 33 years. TIL and ICP were similar in both groups. No significant adverse events were noted. Antisecretory factor significantly reduced 30-day mortality in severe TBI in an LMIC environment. Further studies investigating the mechanisms by which antisecretory factor act are warranted. The safety, easy administration and marked efficacy of antisecretory factor support exploration in multicentre randomized controlled trials.
