摘要要点：该内容涉及小胶质细胞、神经炎症、TREM2、炎症。原文：Neuroinflammation is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease (AD), yet the molecular mechanisms linking microglial dysfunction to amyloid-β (Aβ) pathology remain incompletely understood. Here, we identify galectin-3 (Gal3) as a regulator of plaque-associated microglial responses, linking Aβ aggregation, lysosomal function, and neuritic pathology. Using postmortem brain tissue from AD patients, we found that Gal3+ microglia preferentially associate with cored plaques and correlate with increased LAMP1+ dystrophic neurites. Gal3 was also detected in microglial lysosomal compartments, extramicroglial dystrophic structures, and in association with extracellular amyloid fibrils, suggesting roles in both intracellular and extracellular Aβ processing. In APP mice, Gal3 deficiency reduced plaque burden and neuritic dystrophy, increased plaque compaction, and enhanced TREM2 coverage around amyloid plaques. Mechanistically, in vitro microglial experiments showed that Gal3 modulates Aβ uptake and lysosomal processing, whereas lysosomal dysfunction promoted the accumulation of fibrillar Aβ from initially monomeric species, particularly in Gal3-deficient cells. Cell-free assays demonstrated that Gal3 preferentially binds fibrillar Aβ, inhibits secondary nucleation, and alters fibril morphology, while Gal3-modified fibrillar Aβ reduces N2a neuronal cell number. Finally, in silico structural analyses identified candidate Gal3 interactors enriched in leucine-rich-repeat and immunoglobulin-like proteins, supporting a role for this lectin in immune receptor regulation. Complementary in vivo transcriptomic profiling showed that Gal3 deletion suppresses type I interferon and microglial neurodegenerative transcriptional signatures. Together, these findings position Gal3 at the interface between Aβ remodeling, lysosomal homeostasis, and plaque-associated microglial responses in AD.

摘要要点：该内容涉及小胶质细胞、神经炎症、炎症。原文：Chronic neuroinflammation and oxidative stress are important contributors to Alzheimer's disease (AD). Myeloperoxidase (MPO), a key driver of oxidative inflammation released mainly by activated microglia in the neurodegenerative brain, is a potential therapeutic target to inhibit the neuroinflammatory cascade and oxidative damage in AD, but the underlying mechanisms remain unexplored, and achieving targeted brain delivery of an MPO inhibitor and minimizing systemic effects remain significant challenges. We addressed these limitations by first establishing that genetic deletion of MPO in the 5XFAD mouse model improved memory performance and reduced amyloid deposition, validating MPO as a therapeutic target. We then developed a liposome-encapsulated reactive oxygen species (ROS)-activatable MPO inhibitor (LERAMI). LERAMI uniquely combines targeted delivery with on-demand drug release, utilizing a ROS-sensitive linker to selectively release MPO inhibitors in ROS-rich regions within the AD microenvironment, minimizing off-target effects and systemic toxicity. In the 5XFAD mouse model. By reducing MPO expression and activity, LERAMI significantly improved cognitive performance, accompanied by decreased microglial activation, attenuated oxidative DNA damage, improved synaptic and neuronal density, and reduced amyloid burden. RNA sequencing revealed a robust hippocampal transcriptomic reprogramming, with upregulation of pathways critical for synaptic plasticity, calcium signaling, axon guidance, and neuroprotection, along with downregulation of inflammatory and immune response pathways. These findings demonstrate that targeted ROS-activatable MPO inhibition with LERAMI represents a promising therapeutic strategy for AD and potentially other neuroinflammatory diseases, offering a unique approach to mitigate neuroinflammation, preserve synaptic integrity, and restore cognitive function.

摘要要点：该内容涉及脑损伤、小胶质细胞、神经炎症、炎症。原文：Brain ischemia induces robust neuroinflammation driven by microglial activation, yet whether ischemic neurons actively reprogram microglial phenotypes to limit inflammation remains elusive. Here, we identify neuronal interleukin-34 (IL-34) as a critical immunomodulatory signal that maintains microglial homeostasis after cerebral ischemia. We demonstrate that ischemic injury augments IL-34-CSF1R signaling between neurons and microglia. Neuron-specific overexpression of Il34 (Syn1-Il34tg mice) attenuated infarct volume and neurodeficits after middle cerebral artery occlusion, whereas neuronal Il34 knockdown exacerbated brain injury. Microglial depletion abolished IL-34-mediated neuroprotection, suggesting microglia as essential effectors. Thus, ischemic neurons deploy IL-34 to orchestrate the immunoregulatory activity of microglia via the TGF-β-MEF2C axis, representing a previously unrecognized endogenous mechanism counteracting neuroinflammation. Our study reveals neuronal IL-34 as a potential therapeutic target for ischemic stroke.

摘要要点：该内容涉及细胞外囊泡、干细胞。原文：Wedged between the sarcolemma and basal lamina, satellite cells (SCs) are resident skeletal muscle stem cells responsible for regeneration following injury. SCs share this microenvironment with fibrogenic, immune, vascular, and neural cell populations. Accumulating evidence indicate that SCs do more from this position than regenerate muscle. Activated SCs participate directly in extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling and signal to fibroblasts and fibro-adipogenic progenitors (FAPs) through miRNA-containing extracellular vesicles (EVs). SCs also reside near capillaries, where reciprocal signaling with endothelial cells stimulates angiogenesis and in return influences myofiber adaptation. SCs are also localized at the neuromuscular junction (NMJ), where they secrete proteins implicated in synaptic remodeling. During regeneration, SCs share their environment with infiltrating immune cells, and while the influence of macrophages on SCs is well established, the reciprocal direction remains comparatively unexplored. EV transport has emerged as one candidate mechanism for part of this influence, operating independently of SC-myofiber fusion. However, supporting evidence is stronger for fibroblasts and FAPs than for endothelial cells and is currently absent for NMJ, immune cells, and non-myogenic fate transitions. The consequences of losing SCs are evident in SC depletion models, where muscle develops excess collagen, blunted capillary expansion, and NMJs that regenerate poorly and degenerate prematurely with age. Whether SCs also transition to fibrogenic or adipogenic fates in aged and dystrophic muscle remains less clear. This review highlights non-canonical roles of SCs and evaluates fusion-independent communication, including SC-derived EVs, as a candidate mechanism linking SCs to the fibrogenic, immune, vascular, and neural compartments of muscle.

摘要要点：该内容涉及小胶质细胞、神经炎症、炎症。原文：Neurotropic viruses induce behavioral and cognitive alterations through diverse molecular and cellular mechanisms that extend beyond simple neuronal damage. Comparative analysis of rabies virus (RABV), pseudorabies virus (PRV), Borna disease virus (BoDV-1), SARS-CoV-2, Nipah virus (NiV), West Nile virus (WNV) and Herpes Simplex virus 1 (HSV-1) reveals distinct pathogenic strategies targeting specific neural circuits. While neuroinflammation characterized by microglial activation, astrocytic responses, and pro-inflammatory cytokine production contributes significantly to synaptic and network dysfunction in WNV and NiV infections, other mechanisms predominate in different pathogens. Additionally, systemic factors such as the gut-brain axis and microbiome alterations, particularly evident in SARS-CoV-2 infection, modulate neurotransmitter metabolism and neuroimmune responses. Considering the lack of even diffuse neuronal loss observed during infection, behavioral changes may arise more from selective, circuit-dependent vulnerabilities, involving specific disruptions to emotion, cognition, and motor control networks. To provide a clear review of the knowledge regarding how targeted neuronal population alterations translate into behavioral phenotypes, we here integrate molecular, cellular, and systems-level insights across these seven neurotropic viruses used as models, with the aim of identifying common pathways linking infection to behavioral changes and cognitive dysfunction. Understanding these discrete mechanisms ranging from acute synaptic manipulation to long-lasting neuroimmune dysregulation is essential for developing therapeutic strategies that address both the infectious agent and the specific neural networks mediating acute disturbances and long-term neurological sequelae.
