中文摘要

神经炎症是阿尔茨海默病 (AD) 的一个标志，但将小胶质细胞功能障碍与 β 淀粉样蛋白 (Aβ) 病理学联系起来的分子机制仍不完全清楚。在这里，我们确定半乳糖凝集素 3 (Gal3) 是斑块相关小胶质细胞反应的调节剂，将 Aβ 聚集、溶酶体功能和神经炎病理学联系起来。使用 AD 患者死后脑组织，我们发现 Gal3+ 小胶质细胞优先与核心斑块相关，并与 LAMP1+ 营养不良性神经突增加相关。 Gal3 还在小胶质细胞溶酶体区室、小胶质细胞外营养不良结构中检测到，并与细胞外淀粉样原纤维相关，表明其在细胞内和细胞外 Aβ 加工中发挥作用。在 APP 小鼠中，Gal3 缺乏减少了斑块负担和神经炎性营养不良，增加了斑块压实度，并增强了淀粉样斑块周围 TREM2 的覆盖。从机制上讲，体外小胶质细胞实验表明，Gal3 调节 Aβ 摄取和溶酶体加工，而溶酶体功能障碍促进了最初单体形式的纤维状 Aβ 的积累，特别是在 Gal3 缺陷的细胞中。无细胞测定表明，Gal3 优先结合纤维状 Aβ，抑制二次成核，并改变纤维形态，而 Gal3 修饰的纤维状 Aβ 减少 N2a 神经元细胞数量。最后，通过计算机结构分析确定了富含亮氨酸重复序列和免疫球蛋白样蛋白的候选 Gal3 相互作用蛋白，支持了这种凝集素在免疫受体调节中的作用。补充体内转录组分析表明 Gal3 缺失抑制 I 型干扰素和小胶质细胞神经退行性转录特征。总之，这些发现将 Gal3 定位在 AD 中 Aβ 重塑、溶酶体稳态和斑块相关小胶质细胞反应之间的界面。

英文摘要

Neuroinflammation is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease (AD), yet the molecular mechanisms linking microglial dysfunction to amyloid-β (Aβ) pathology remain incompletely understood. Here, we identify galectin-3 (Gal3) as a regulator of plaque-associated microglial responses, linking Aβ aggregation, lysosomal function, and neuritic pathology. Using postmortem brain tissue from AD patients, we found that Gal3+ microglia preferentially associate with cored plaques and correlate with increased LAMP1+ dystrophic neurites. Gal3 was also detected in microglial lysosomal compartments, extramicroglial dystrophic structures, and in association with extracellular amyloid fibrils, suggesting roles in both intracellular and extracellular Aβ processing. In APP mice, Gal3 deficiency reduced plaque burden and neuritic dystrophy, increased plaque compaction, and enhanced TREM2 coverage around amyloid plaques. Mechanistically, in vitro microglial experiments showed that Gal3 modulates Aβ uptake and lysosomal processing, whereas lysosomal dysfunction promoted the accumulation of fibrillar Aβ from initially monomeric species, particularly in Gal3-deficient cells. Cell-free assays demonstrated that Gal3 preferentially binds fibrillar Aβ, inhibits secondary nucleation, and alters fibril morphology, while Gal3-modified fibrillar Aβ reduces N2a neuronal cell number. Finally, in silico structural analyses identified candidate Gal3 interactors enriched in leucine-rich-repeat and immunoglobulin-like proteins, supporting a role for this lectin in immune receptor regulation. Complementary in vivo transcriptomic profiling showed that Gal3 deletion suppresses type I interferon and microglial neurodegenerative transcriptional signatures. Together, these findings position Gal3 at the interface between Aβ remodeling, lysosomal homeostasis, and plaque-associated microglial responses in AD.

Chronic neuroinflammation and oxidative stress are important contributors to Alzheimer's disease (AD). Myeloperoxidase (MPO), a key driver of oxidative inflammation released mainly by activated microglia in the neurodegenerative brain, is a potential therapeutic target to inhibit the neuroinflammatory cascade and oxidative damage in AD, but the underlying mechanisms remain unexplored, and achieving targeted brain delivery of an MPO inhibitor and minimizing systemic effects remain significant challenges. We addressed these limitations by first establishing that genetic deletion of MPO in the 5XFAD mouse model improved memory performance and reduced amyloid deposition, validating MPO as a therapeutic target. We then developed a liposome-encapsulated reactive oxygen species (ROS)-activatable MPO inhibitor (LERAMI). LERAMI uniquely combines targeted delivery with on-demand drug release, utilizing a ROS-sensitive linker to selectively release MPO inhibitors in ROS-rich regions within the AD microenvironment, minimizing off-target effects and systemic toxicity. In the 5XFAD mouse model. By reducing MPO expression and activity, LERAMI significantly improved cognitive performance, accompanied by decreased microglial activation, attenuated oxidative DNA damage, improved synaptic and neuronal density, and reduced amyloid burden. RNA sequencing revealed a robust hippocampal transcriptomic reprogramming, with upregulation of pathways critical for synaptic plasticity, calcium signaling, axon guidance, and neuroprotection, along with downregulation of inflammatory and immune response pathways. These findings demonstrate that targeted ROS-activatable MPO inhibition with LERAMI represents a promising therapeutic strategy for AD and potentially other neuroinflammatory diseases, offering a unique approach to mitigate neuroinflammation, preserve synaptic integrity, and restore cognitive function.

中文摘要

英文摘要

Brain ischemia induces robust neuroinflammation driven by microglial activation, yet whether ischemic neurons actively reprogram microglial phenotypes to limit inflammation remains elusive. Here, we identify neuronal interleukin-34 (IL-34) as a critical immunomodulatory signal that maintains microglial homeostasis after cerebral ischemia. We demonstrate that ischemic injury augments IL-34-CSF1R signaling between neurons and microglia. Neuron-specific overexpression of Il34 (Syn1-Il34tg mice) attenuated infarct volume and neurodeficits after middle cerebral artery occlusion, whereas neuronal Il34 knockdown exacerbated brain injury. Microglial depletion abolished IL-34-mediated neuroprotection, suggesting microglia as essential effectors. Single-cell RNA sequencing revealed that neuronal IL-34 preserved homeostatic microglial signatures (P2ry12, Cx3cr1, Tgfbr1), while suppressing inflammatory activities (Il1b, Tnf, Cxcl2) through upregulation of the transcription factor MEF2C. Mechanistically, IL-34 activated TGF-β signaling in microglia and inhibition of TGF-β signaling abolished the protective effects of IL-34, whereas microglia-specific Mef2c overexpression rescued neuroprotection despite TGF-β blockade. Thus, ischemic neurons deploy IL-34 to orchestrate the immunoregulatory activity of microglia via the TGF-β-MEF2C axis, representing a previously unrecognized endogenous mechanism counteracting neuroinflammation. Our study reveals neuronal IL-34 as a potential therapeutic target for ischemic stroke.

中文摘要

多发性硬化症（MS）是一种慢性中枢神经系统疾病，其特征是复杂的病理学，涉及重叠的免疫介导的炎症和进行性神经变性，两者都会导致累积性残疾。尽管多发性硬化症研究和管理取得了巨大进展，但能够捕获疾病活动、进展和治疗反应的可靠生物标志物仍然是一个重要的未满足需求。这篇综述批判性地综合了反映免疫激活、神经轴突损伤、神经胶质功能障碍、氧化应激以及分子和代谢改变的生物标志物的当前证据。在目前研究的液体生物标志物中，神经丝轻链是神经轴突损伤和炎症性疾病活动最广泛验证的标志物，而神经胶质纤维酸性蛋白提供有关星形细胞病理学和疾病进展的补充信息。基于脂质组学和代谢组学、细胞外囊泡、转录组学和表观遗传分析、微小RNA和肠道微生物组相关特征的新兴方法可能会进一步改善复发性和进展性多发性硬化症的表征。实验模型被认为是补充转化平台，用于将可测量的生物标志物变化与炎症、组织损伤、代谢功能障碍和治疗反应的明确机制联系起来，同时区分机械合理性与临床生物标志物性能。鉴于多发性硬化症的生物异质性，未来的进展可能取决于综合的、多区室和多组学的生物标志物特征与临床和影像学测量相结合，而不是仅仅依赖于单个标志物。大型纵向队列、分析标准化、独立验证以及跨 MS 表型和治疗设置的评估对于将这些方法转化为临床上有用的工具来监测疾病活动和进展、预后、患者分层和个体化管理至关重要。

英文摘要

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a chronic central nervous system disorder characterized by complex pathobiology involving overlapping immune-mediated inflammation and progressive neurodegeneration, both contributing to cumulative disability. Despite substantial advances in MS research and management, reliable biomarkers capable of capturing disease activity, progression, and therapeutic response remain an important unmet need. This review critically synthesizes current evidence on biomarkers reflecting immune activation, neuroaxonal injury, glial dysfunction, oxidative stress, and molecular and metabolic alterations. Among currently investigated fluid biomarkers, neurofilament light chain is the most extensively validated marker of neuroaxonal injury and inflammatory disease activity, whereas glial fibrillary acidic protein provides complementary information on astrocytic pathology and disease progression. Emerging approaches based on lipidomics and metabolomics, extracellular vesicles, transcriptomic and epigenetic profiling, microRNAs, and gut microbiome-associated signatures may further improve characterization of relapsing and progressive MS. Experimental models are considered as complementary translational platforms for linking measurable biomarker changes with defined mechanisms of inflammation, tissue injury, metabolic dysfunction, and treatment response, while distinguishing mechanistic plausibility from clinical biomarker performance. Given the biological heterogeneity of MS, future progress is likely to depend on integrated, multi-compartment and multi-omic biomarker signatures combined with clinical and imaging measures rather than on individual markers alone. Large longitudinal cohorts, analytical standardization, independent validation, and assessment across MS phenotypes and treatment settings will be essential for translating these approaches into clinically useful tools for monitoring disease activity and progression, prognosis, patient stratification, and individualized management.

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Immune dysfunction is hypothesised to underlie psychotic disorders. To test this, we assessed cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) cytokine levels and their relationship with symptoms and a brain imaging marker of 18-kDa translocator protein (TSPO), a protein highly expressed by microglia in neuroinflammatory conditions. METHODS: A case-control study was performed including patients with schizophrenia or related psychotic disorders with <5 years of illness duration and psychotic symptoms despite antipsychotic treatment, and matched healthy volunteers. All participants received clinical interviews and lumbar punctures, with 84% also receiving [18F]DPA-714 positron emission tomography (PET) scans to quantify relative cortical grey matter TSPO levels. CSF levels of IFN-γ, IL-1β, IL-2, IL-4, IL-6, IL-8, IL-10, IL-12p70, IL-13, TNF-α were measured. RESULTS: Forty-three patients and 25 healthy controls participated. Patients had significantly higher CSF levels of TNF-α (p < 0.001), IL-8 (p = 0.015), IL-10 (p = 0.032), and IL-13 (p = 0.002) relative to controls with moderate to large effect sizes. There were no significant correlations between symptom measures and CSF cytokine levels after correcting for multiple comparisons (all p > 0.05). Furthermore, CSF cytokine levels were not associated with PET TSPO levels in cortical grey matter regions (all p > 0.05). CONCLUSIONS: Early course schizophrenia is associated with generalised dysregulation of CSF cytokines, consistent with immune dysregulation in psychosis. The lack of association with PET TSPO levels suggests this is unlikely to be related to microglial changes. CLINICALTRIALS: gov: NCT03093064.
