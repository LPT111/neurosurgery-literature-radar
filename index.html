PMID: 43062771 ｜ DOI: 10.1007/s12015-026-11274-7

中文摘要

炎症性肠病（IBD），包括克罗恩病和溃疡性结肠炎，是一种慢性、复发性胃肠道疾病，相当一部分患者对当前的治疗没有反应，这凸显了对新策略的需求。间充质基质细胞（MSC）及其衍生物由于其自我更新能力、多谱系分化和有效的免疫调节特性，已成为再生医学的一个有前途的前沿领域。 MSC 通过抑制效应 T 细胞反应、扩大调节性 T (Treg) 和 B (Breg) 细胞、使巨噬细胞极化至抗炎表型、减少促炎细胞因子和塑造肠道微生物群来调节肠道微环境。大多数这些作用是由 MSC 分泌组和细胞外囊泡 (EV) 携带的旁分泌信号介导的，它们传递细胞因子、微小 RNA 和蛋白质，促进组织修复、血管生成和炎症消退。基于条件培养基或外泌体的无细胞方法比直接细胞移植具有更多优势，包括提高安全性、更容易标准化和降低免疫原性。源自骨髓、脂肪组织和围产期组织的间充质干细胞均表现出独特的生物学和治疗特征。克罗恩病复杂肛周瘘的治疗是临床上最先进的应用，darvadstrocel 在临床试验中证明了安全性和有效性。其他新兴适应症包括肠纤维化和组织再生。主要挑战仍然存在，包括细胞异质性、缺乏标准化制造以及剂量和给药的优化。未来的前景指向从基于细胞的疗法到下一代无细胞策略的范式转变，包括分泌组衍生制剂和工程细胞外囊泡，这可能为 IBD 患者提供更安全、更可扩展且最终改善疾病的治疗选择。

英文摘要

Inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD), including Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis, are chronic, relapsing disorders of the gastrointestinal tract for which a substantial proportion of patients fail to respond to current therapies, underscoring the need for novel strategies. Mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) and their derivatives have emerged as a promising frontier in regenerative medicine, owing to their self-renewal capacity, multilineage differentiation, and potent immunomodulatory properties. MSCs modulate the intestinal microenvironment by suppressing effector T-cell responses, expanding regulatory T (Treg) and B (Breg) cells, polarizing macrophages toward anti-inflammatory phenotypes, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines, and shaping the gut microbiota. Most of these effects are mediated by paracrine signals carried by the MSC secretome and extracellular vesicles (EVs), which deliver cytokines, microRNAs, and proteins that promote tissue repair, angiogenesis, and resolution of inflammation. Cell-free approaches based on conditioned medium or exosomes offer further advantages over direct cell transplantation, including improved safety, easier standardization, and reduced immunogenicity. MSCs derived from bone marrow, adipose tissue, and perinatal tissues each show distinct biological and therapeutic features. The treatment of complex perianal fistulas in Crohn's disease is the most clinically advanced application, with darvadstrocel demonstrating safety and efficacy in clinical trials. Additional emerging indications include intestinal fibrosis and tissue regeneration. Key challenges remain, including cellular heterogeneity, lack of standardized manufacturing, and optimization of dosing and delivery. Future perspectives point toward a paradigm shift from cell-based therapies to next generation cell-free strategies, including secretome-derived formulations and engineered extracellular vescicles, which may provide safer, more scalable and ultimately disease-modifying therapeutic options for patients with IBD.

英文摘要

Neuroinflammation is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease (AD), yet the molecular mechanisms linking microglial dysfunction to amyloid-β (Aβ) pathology remain incompletely understood. Here, we identify galectin-3 (Gal3) as a regulator of plaque-associated microglial responses, linking Aβ aggregation, lysosomal function, and neuritic pathology. Using postmortem brain tissue from AD patients, we found that Gal3+ microglia preferentially associate with cored plaques and correlate with increased LAMP1+ dystrophic neurites. Gal3 was also detected in microglial lysosomal compartments, extramicroglial dystrophic structures, and in association with extracellular amyloid fibrils, suggesting roles in both intracellular and extracellular Aβ processing. In APP mice, Gal3 deficiency reduced plaque burden and neuritic dystrophy, increased plaque compaction, and enhanced TREM2 coverage around amyloid plaques. Mechanistically, in vitro microglial experiments showed that Gal3 modulates Aβ uptake and lysosomal processing, whereas lysosomal dysfunction promoted the accumulation of fibrillar Aβ from initially monomeric species, particularly in Gal3-deficient cells. Cell-free assays demonstrated that Gal3 preferentially binds fibrillar Aβ, inhibits secondary nucleation, and alters fibril morphology, while Gal3-modified fibrillar Aβ reduces N2a neuronal cell number. Finally, in silico structural analyses identified candidate Gal3 interactors enriched in leucine-rich-repeat and immunoglobulin-like proteins, supporting a role for this lectin in immune receptor regulation. Complementary in vivo transcriptomic profiling showed that Gal3 deletion suppresses type I interferon and microglial neurodegenerative transcriptional signatures. Together, these findings position Gal3 at the interface between Aβ remodeling, lysosomal homeostasis, and plaque-associated microglial responses in AD.

Here, we identify neuronal interleukin-34 (IL-34) as a critical immunomodulatory signal that maintains microglial homeostasis after cerebral ischemia. Thus, ischemic neurons deploy IL-34 to orchestrate the immunoregulatory activity of microglia via the TGF-β-MEF2C axis, representing a previously unrecognized endogenous mechanism counteracting neuroinflammation. Our study reveals neuronal IL-34 as a potential therapeutic target for ischemic stroke.

Conventional surgical and medical interventions have not been sufficient to prevent long-term disability, prompting investigation into novel regenerative strategies. We conducted a narrative review of preclinical and limited clinical evidence on stem cell- and extracellular vesicle/exosome-based therapies for SAH, with emphasis on MSCs and their derivatives. PubMed/MEDLINE and Embase were searched between February and March 2025 for studies examining mechanisms, therapeutic targets, dosing, timing, and routes of administration. Stem cell-based therapies, particularly MSCs and their exosomes, have shown the ability to attenuate neuroinflammation, oxidative stress, excitotoxicity, and BBB disruption. Key mechanisms include modulation of the HMGB1/TLR4/NF-κB axis, polarization of microglia toward an anti-inflammatory phenotype, and secretion of trophic factors such as TSG-6, BDNF, and NT-3.
