中文摘要

小胶质细胞功能障碍越来越多地与阿尔茨海默病 (AD) 发病机制相关，小胶质细胞受体 TREM2 的功能丧失变异是迟发性 AD 的主要危险因素。我们报告了 VG-3927，一种口服中枢神经系统渗透性小分子 TREM2 激动剂，它与 TREM2 及其接头 DAP12 的跨膜结构域结合，促进高阶受体复合物的形成和下游信号传导。 VG-3927 和工具化合物 VG-3909 表现出（亚）纳摩尔效力，与包括淀粉样蛋白-β (Aβ) 在内的内源配体配合，并恢复疾病相关 TREM2 变体中的信号传导。在人 iPSC 衍生的神经元-小胶质细胞-星形胶质细胞培养物中，VG-3927 减少了炎症细胞因子和神经元损伤标志物。在表达 hTREM2 常见变体 (5xFAD;hTREM2CV) 的 5xFAD 淀粉样变性小鼠中，VG-3909 诱导小胶质细胞的神经保护性转录重编程，特别是在斑块生态位中；增强 Aβ 吞噬作用；并促进大脑葡萄糖代谢。相比之下，可能被可溶性 TREM2 (sTREM2) 隔离的 TREM2 激动剂抗体未能与斑块小胶质细胞结合。在一项首次人体、随机、双盲、安慰剂对照 1 期试验（NCT06343636，n = 115）中，VG-3927 在单次和多次递增剂量（高达 140 毫克）中具有良好的耐受性。药代动力学支持每日一次给药，具有高中枢神经系统渗透性（脑脊液与未结合血浆的比率为 0.91）。 VG-3927 诱导 CSF sTREM2（一种靶点参与的生物标志物）呈剂量依赖性减少，在健康和老年参与者以及 AD 参与者中一致，支持 AD 的进一步临床开发，新名称为 SAR448851。

英文摘要

Microglial dysfunction is increasingly implicated in Alzheimer's disease (AD) pathogenesis, with loss-of-function variants in the microglial receptor TREM2 as major risk factors for late-onset AD. We report VG-3927, an oral, CNS-penetrant small-molecule TREM2 agonist that engages the transmembrane domains of TREM2 and its adaptor DAP12 to promote higher-order receptor complex formation and downstream signaling. VG-3927 and tool compound VG-3909 exhibited (sub)nanomolar potency, cooperated with endogenous ligands including amyloid-β (Aβ), and restored signaling in disease-associated TREM2 variants. In human iPSC-derived neuron-microglia-astrocyte cultures, VG-3927 reduced inflammatory cytokines and markers of neuronal damage. In 5xFAD amyloidosis mice expressing the common variant of hTREM2 (5xFAD;hTREM2CV), VG-3909 induced neuroprotective transcriptional reprogramming of microglia, particularly in the plaque niche; enhanced Aβ phagocytosis; and boosted brain glucose metabolism. By contrast, a TREM2 agonist antibody, which may be sequestered by soluble TREM2 (sTREM2), failed to engage plaque microglia. In a first-in-human, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 1 trial (NCT06343636, n = 115), VG-3927 was well tolerated across single and multiple ascending doses of up to 140 milligrams. Pharmacokinetics supported once-daily dosing with high CNS penetration (CSF-to-unbound plasma ratio of 0.91). VG-3927 induced dose-dependent reductions in CSF sTREM2, a biomarker of target engagement, consistent across healthy and elderly participants and participants with AD, supporting further clinical development in AD under the new designation SAR448851.

英文摘要

Herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1) is a neurotropic pathogen capable of invading the central nervous system (CNS) and is associated with chronic neuroinflammation, cognitive impairment, and neurodegenerative disease. While microglia orchestrate the initial immune response to HSV‑1, the molecular mechanisms that regulate their sustained neuroinflammatory activity in vivo remain poorly understood. To define the transcriptional and epigenetic mechanisms that shape microglial responses during acute HSV-1 infection in vivo, we have, for the first time, integrated single‑nucleus RNA sequencing, chromatin accessibility profiling, and spatial proteomics and transcriptomics in an intranasal HSV‑1 infection model. Single-cell multiome analysis of CD11b⁺ nuclei identified transcriptionally and epigenetically distinct microglial and macrophage populations. HSV-1 infection redistributed monocyte-lineage states, with a marked overrepresentation of interferon (IFN)‑responsive microglia and macrophage-associated populations characterized by amplification of Stat1/2, Irf1, and Cebpb rather than reflecting uniform inflammatory activation. Complementary spatial proteomics further confirmed increased microglial activation and astrocytic responses specifically within HSV-1 antigen-positive regions. Together, these integrated multiomic analyses demonstrate that HSV-1 induces localized transcriptional and epigenetic remodeling of microglia dominated by IFN-driven regulatory programs and a loss of homeostatic signatures. By linking single-cell regulatory architecture to spatially restricted tissue responses, our findings provide mechanistic insight into how localized viral infection sustains neuroinflammation, contributing to long-term neurological vulnerability and neurodegenerative disease risk.

英文摘要

Growing evidence has demonstrated a significant association between reduced cerebral blood flow and Alzheimer's disease (AD)-related cognitive impairment. Chronic cerebral hypoperfusion (CCH) has been reported to exacerbate tau hyperphosphorylation and behavioral deficits in rodents, but whether CCH promotes tau pathology at the initial stage of tauopathy remains unclear. Here we show, using a tau-seeded PS19 mouse model with unilateral common carotid artery occlusion, that early CCH exacerbates spatially biased tau pathology, inducing ipsilateral enrichment of insoluble tau in the hippocampus and, in the cortex, an ipsilateral increase that was dependent on the presence of tau seeds. We evaluate its dual utility as a clinical biomarker and therapeutic target, addressing challenges in brain delivery, target selectivity, and safety. 中文摘要

缺血性中风后，神经炎症会导致严重的继发性免疫病理学，但全身干预措施如何协调外周和中枢免疫以实现神经保护仍然难以捉摸。在这里，我们证明急性期运动通过激活下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺（HPA）轴作为有效的全身断路器来加速运动康复和神经修复。从系统角度来看，通过靶向受体阻断验证的运动驱动型糖皮质激素可诱导严重的脾萎缩，从而限制外周免疫池，有效地剥夺缺血性脑部的浸润白细胞。通过利用高分辨率单细胞转录组学，我们确定小胶质细胞是这种糖皮质激素介导的免疫抑制的主要、高度敏感的中央传感器。这些内分泌重编程的小胶质细胞从高度促炎状态转变为受调节的稳态静止状态。从机制上讲，它们决定性地削弱干扰素 (IFN) 信号传导，从而消除 CXCL9/10-CXCR3 趋化性、减弱抗原呈递和共刺激，从而阻止效应 T 和 B 细胞的募集和原位激活。此外，这种对神经炎症的早期抑制限制了神经组织损伤，将更广泛的神经胶质细胞生态位保留在静止的生理状态。因此，通常由小胶质细胞向星形胶质细胞和少突胶质细胞提供的反应性修复信号显着减弱，这强调了早期保护神经炎症有效地消除了强烈代偿性修复的需要。总的来说，我们的研究结果描绘了一个新的全身到中枢神经免疫轴。通过展示运动如何利用全身内分泌信号来中断病理性炎症级联反应，我们强调小胶质细胞糖皮质激素信号传导是中风后神经免疫调节的精确治疗靶点。

英文摘要

Following ischemic stroke, neuroinflammation drives severe secondary immunopathology, yet how systemic interventions coordinate peripheral and central immunity for neuroprotection remains elusive. Here, we demonstrate that acute-phase exercise accelerates motor rehabilitation and neural repair by activating the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis to function as a potent systemic circuit breaker. Systemically, exercise-driven glucocorticoids, validated via targeted receptor blockade, induce profound spleen atrophy to restrict the peripheral immune pool, effectively depriving the ischemic brain of infiltrating leukocytes. By leveraging high-resolution single-cell transcriptomics, we identify microglia as the primary, highly sensitive central sensors of this glucocorticoid-mediated immunosuppression. These endocrine-reprogrammed microglia transition from a highly pro-inflammatory state toward a regulated, homeostatic quiescence. Mechanistically, they decisively blunt interferon (IFN) signaling to abrogate CXCL9/10-CXCR3 chemotaxis, attenuate antigen presentation and co-stimulation, thereby arresting the recruitment and in situ activation of effector T and B cells. Furthermore, this early suppression of neuroinflammation limits neural tissue damage, preserving the broader neuroglial niche in a quiescent, physiological state. Accordingly, the reactive repair signals typically provided by microglia to astrocytes and oligodendrocytes are substantially attenuated, underscoring that early protection from neuroinflammation effectively obviates the need for intense compensatory repair. Collectively, our findings delineate a novel systemic-to-central neuroimmune axis. By demonstrating how exercise harnesses systemic endocrine signals to interrupt the pathological inflammatory cascade, we highlight microglial glucocorticoid signaling as a precise therapeutic target for post-stroke neuroimmune modulation.
