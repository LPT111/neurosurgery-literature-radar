阿尔茨海默病 (AD) 是导致痴呆症的主要原因，人们越来越认识到这是一种代谢紊乱，其特征是大脑中胰岛素和胰岛素样生长因子 1 信号传导中断，这一概念现在被称为 3 型糖尿病。这一观点的核心是胰岛素受体底物 1/磷酸肌醇 3-激酶/蛋白激酶 B/雷帕霉素机械靶标 (IRS-1/PI3K/Akt/mTOR) 级联，它是连接全身胰岛素抵抗与神经变性的主要汇聚点。星形胶质细胞、神经元、小胶质细胞和脑内皮细胞中的胰岛素信号受损会破坏葡萄糖代谢、能量产生和淀粉样蛋白-β (Aβ) 清除，同时通过糖原合成酶激酶 3β 激活促进 tau 过度磷酸化。慢性 mTOR 过度激活会抑制自噬，损害胰岛素敏感性，并通过紧密连接解体、周细胞丢失和内皮衰老损害血脑屏障 (BBB) 完整性，从而加剧这些功能障碍。这些过程共同建立了代谢功能障碍、蛋白质聚集、神经炎症和血管破裂的自我延续循环。神经元衍生的细胞外囊泡已成为一种有前途的非侵入性生物标志物平台，用于实时监测中枢胰岛素抵抗和治疗反应。代谢靶向干预措施，包括鼻内胰岛素、胰高血糖素样肽 1 受体激动剂和雷帕霉素等 mTOR 抑制剂，在恢复胰岛素敏感性、减少 tau 和 Aβ 病理以及保留 BBB 功能方面表现出令人鼓舞的临床前和早期临床疗效。然而，与给药和剂量相关的挑战仍然是临床转化的重大障碍。对神经血管单元和多靶点联合疗法的持续研究可能有助于开发有效的、生物标志物引导的 AD 治疗方法。

英文摘要

Alzheimer's disease (AD), the leading cause of dementia, is increasingly recognized as a metabolic disorder characterized by disrupted insulin and insulin-like growth factor-1 signaling in the brain, a concept now referred to as type 3 diabetes. Central to this perspective is the insulin receptor substrate-1/phosphoinositide 3-kinase/protein kinase B/mechanistic target of rapamycin (IRS-1/PI3K/Akt/mTOR) cascade, which acts as the primary convergence point linking systemic insulin resistance to neurodegeneration. Impaired insulin signaling in astrocytes, neurons, microglia, and cerebral endothelial cells disrupts glucose metabolism, energy production, and amyloid-β (Aβ) clearance, while promoting tau hyperphosphorylation through glycogen synthase kinase-3β activation. Chronic mTOR hyperactivation exacerbates these dysfunctions by suppressing autophagy, impairing insulin sensitivity, and compromising blood-brain barrier (BBB) integrity through tight junction disassembly, pericyte loss, and endothelial senescence. These processes collectively establish a self-perpetuating cycle of metabolic dysfunction, protein aggregation, neuroinflammation, and vascular breakdown. Neuron-derived extracellular vesicles have emerged as a promising non-invasive biomarker platform for monitoring central insulin resistance and therapeutic response in real time. Metabolically targeted interventions, including intranasal insulin, glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists, and mTOR inhibitors such as rapamycin, demonstrate encouraging preclinical and early clinical efficacy in restoring insulin sensitivity, reducing tau and Aβ pathology, and preserving BBB function. However, challenges related to delivery and dosing remain significant barriers to clinical translation. Continued investigation into neurovascular unit and multi-target combination therapies may facilitate the development of effective, biomarker-guided treatments for AD.

Adrenomedullin (ADM), a vasoactive member of the calcitonin peptide family with receptor-signalling overlap with calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP), has emerged as a candidate therapeutic target in migraine. Given the striking clinical parallels between migraine and persistent post-traumatic headache (PTH), we investigated whether ADM-responsive signalling contributes to migraine-like headache in adults with persistent PTH attributed to mild traumatic brain injury. We conducted a single-centre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-way crossover study in adults with persistent PTH and no pre-trauma history of migraine. On two experimental days separated by at least 7 days, participants received 20-minute intravenous infusions of ADM (19.9 pmol kg⁻¹ min⁻¹) and isotonic saline as the matched inactive comparator (placebo) in randomised sequence. Headache outcomes were recorded for 12 hours. The primary outcome was the within-participant incidence of experimentally induced migraine-like headache. The secondary outcome was the within-participant difference in baseline-corrected area under the curve (AUC) for headache intensity from 0 to 12 hours. Of 158 individuals assessed for eligibility, 21 were randomised, completed both periods and were analysed. Participants had a mean age of 34.3 years, 19 (90%) were female, and 18 (86%) reported a migraine-like phenotype. Migraine-like headache occurred in 17 of 21 participants (81%) after ADM and four of 21 (19%) following placebo. Fourteen participants developed migraine-like only after ADM and one only following placebo, yielding a paired absolute risk difference of 61.9 percentage points (95% CI 29.7-80.6; one-sided P < 0.001; two-sided P = 0.001). Baseline-corrected headache-intensity AUC was higher after ADM than placebo (median 1,110 [IQR 315-2205] versus 420 [-160 to 565]; P = 0.006). Median time to onset of migraine-like headache was 30 minutes after ADM and 390 minutes following placebo. Fifteen participants reported that the ADM-induced headache resembled their usual migraine-like headache episodes, and rescue medication was used by 10 participants after ADM and two after placebo. These findings identify pronounced responsiveness to ADM-linked signalling in persistent PTH and support the ADM pathway as a candidate target for mechanism-based therapeutic development, pending confirmation in independent cohorts and complementary preclinical studies.

英文摘要

Emerging evidence increasingly implicates chronic periodontal disease as a contributor to age-related cognitive decline and dementia, including Alzheimer's disease (AD). Periodontitis is defined by persistent oral microbial dysbiosis, ulcerated gingival epithelium, repeated bacteremia and sustained systemic inflammatory signaling - factors that plausibly influence neuroimmune pathways and cerebrovascular integrity. This review synthesizes findings from epidemiological studies, systematic reviews, mechanistic analyses and experimental models to examine the biological links between oral inflammation and neurodegeneration. Although causal inference remains limited by the predominance of observational data, emerging intervention studies and mechanistic evidence suggest that periodontal health is a potentially modifiable factor in dementia prevention strategies.
