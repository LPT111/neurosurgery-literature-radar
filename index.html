中文摘要

综述目的：人们越来越认识到外伤性脑损伤（TBI）是导致死亡和长期残疾的脑血管并发症的危险因素。本综述总结了有关TBI后脑血管并发症的机制、临床表现、诊断和治疗的现有证据，包括创伤后脑梗死、钝性脑血管损伤、创伤后血管痉挛、迟发性脑缺血和脑静脉窦血栓形成。最新发现：最近的研究提高了人们对内皮功能障碍、神经炎症、血脑屏障破坏、凝血功能障碍和大脑自动调节受损在 TBI 后脑血管损伤发病机制中的作用的认识。当代创伤和中风指南越来越支持系统血管成像、多学科决策和个体化抗血栓策略。脑血管并发症的早期识别需要进行系列神经学评估、有针对性的血管成像和生理监测。管理应以特定血管表型为指导，同时平衡缺血和出血风险。未来的前瞻性研究需要标准化诊断标准、完善监测策略并优化机制特异性疗法。

英文摘要

PURPOSE OF REVIEW: Traumatic brain injury (TBI) is increasingly recognized as a risk factor for cerebrovascular complications that contribute to mortality and long-term disability. This review summarizes current evidence regarding the mechanisms, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and management of cerebrovascular complications after TBI, including post-traumatic cerebral infarction, blunt cerebrovascular injury, post-traumatic vasospasm, delayed cerebral ischemia, and cerebral venous sinus thrombosis. RECENT FINDINGS: Recent studies have improved understanding of the roles of endothelial dysfunction, neuroinflammation, blood-brain barrier disruption, coagulopathy, and impaired cerebral autoregulation in the pathogenesis of cerebrovascular injury after TBI. Contemporary trauma and stroke guidelines increasingly support systematic vascular imaging, multidisciplinary decision-making, and individualized antithrombotic strategies. Early identification of cerebrovascular complications requires serial neurological assessments, targeted vascular imaging, and physiologic monitoring. Management should be guided by the specific vascular phenotype while balancing ischemic and hemorrhagic risks. Future prospective studies are needed to standardize diagnostic criteria, refine surveillance strategies, and optimize mechanism-specific therapies.

英文摘要

Autologous plasma-based therapies have emerged as an expanding domain within hematology, transfusion medicine, and regenerative therapeutics. These interventions, including platelet-rich plasma, platelet lysate, and plasma-derived extracellular vesicles (exosomes), leverage biologically active components of a patient's own blood to promote tissue repair and modulate inflammation. Platelets serve as reservoirs of growth factors and cytokines, while plasma-derived extracellular vesicles act as intercellular signaling mediators capable of influencing gene expression and immune responses. This review provides a comprehensive evaluation of the mechanistic rationale, clinical applications, safety profile, and regulatory considerations surrounding these autologous biologics. Evidence from randomized trials and meta-analyses supports the efficacy of platelet-rich plasma in selected musculoskeletal conditions, particularly knee osteoarthritis, while platelet lysate has demonstrated promise in ocular surface disease, wound healing, and degenerative joint disorders. Plasma-derived exosomes represent a rapidly evolving frontier, with encouraging early-phase data but a need for rigorous clinical standardization and controlled trials remain necessary. Although these therapies are generally well tolerated due to their autologous origin, heterogeneity in preparation methods and limited regulatory harmonization remain significant challenges. As the boundaries between classical transfusion practices and advanced biologic therapies continue to expand, autologous plasma-derived interventions exemplify a translational bridge between hemotherapy and regenerative medicine. Continued standardization, high-quality clinical trials, and regulatory oversight are essential to define their role within evidence-based clinical practice.
